How I approach access

Lessons from building these labs.

Model the business first

I map who needs what, at what level and why. Then every group, folder and policy decision gets simpler.

Check before enforcing

I run Conditional Access in report-only mode and read the sign-in logs before turning a policy on, so nobody gets locked out by surprise.

Automate the "remove" side

Most access creep comes from movers and leavers who keep old groups. Offboarding deserves the same automation as onboarding.
